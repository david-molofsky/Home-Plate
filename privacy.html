Privacy Policy

Home Plate · Effective & last updated: 12 August 2026

Overview

Home Plate is a household meal-planning app. It has no backend server: all of your meals, plans, and shopping lists are stored locally on your device using your browser's on-device storage (IndexedDB). We do not operate any servers that receive, store, or process your meal-planning data.

What Data We Collect

  • Meal, planner, and shopping list data — stored entirely on your device. This information is never transmitted to us and we have no ability to access it.
  • Google Drive data (optional) — if you enable household sync, Home Plate uses Google's drive.file scope, which only grants access to files that Home Plate itself creates in your Drive. We cannot see, read, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
  • No accounts, no analytics. Home Plate does not require account creation and does not currently use any analytics or tracking SDKs.

Household Sync via Google Drive

Home Plate supports syncing data across household members using a shared household code and Google Drive as the storage/sync mechanism, rather than individual user accounts. Enabling sync is optional and requires you to sign in with Google and grant the limited drive.file scope described above. Data stored in your Google Drive is also subject to Google's own Privacy Policy.

Advertising & Cookies planned

Home Plate does not currently display advertising. We intend to introduce ads via Google AdSense in a future update to support continued development. When enabled, AdSense may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, which may include personalized advertising depending on your settings. This may involve Google and its partners collecting and using data as described in Google's advertising policy. You will be able to manage ad personalization through your Google account's Ads Settings. This policy will be updated with any additional detail at the time ads are enabled.

Children's Privacy

Home Plate includes a feature allowing meals to be tagged "Kids," "Adults," or "Both" for household meal-planning purposes. This is a household categorization label only — it does not involve collecting information about, or creating accounts for, any child. Home Plate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you clear your browser/app storage or uninstall the app. If you've enabled Google Drive sync, data stored in Drive can be deleted at any time directly through Google Drive or by revoking Home Plate's access in your Google Account permissions.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Home Plate's features evolve. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "last updated" date at the top of this page.
